## 1. REGN: 51.3%

Model 50.3%, debate adjustment +2.0 pts; leaning bull.

- **Thesis (bull, score 7.6):** Dupixent-driven Sanofi collaboration step-change: Q3 2026 Sanofi collaboration revenue rose to $2,724.0 million from $1,617.0 million (+68%), with Regeneron's share of profits at $2,574.9 million vs $1,455.5 million. Dupixent and Kevzara net product sales grew to $6,796.1 million from $5,011.0 million. The Sanofi Development Balance was fully repaid as of the end of Q2 2026 and no longer reduced collaboration revenue beginning in Q3 2026. That structural uplift should carry into the next reported quarter and could set up favorable year-over-year comparisons within 90 days. [E7, E11, E9]
- **Main risk (bear, score 6.5):** Concentration risk has intensified. Sanofi collaboration revenue, mostly Dupixent profit share, rose to 50% of total revenues for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, versus 41% a year earlier. That leaves REGN's earnings increasingly hostage to a single partnered asset. The legacy franchise is also eroding: EYLEA U.S. sales are declining on biosimilar competition after EYLEA lost U.S. exclusivity, and Bayer collaboration revenue fell (23%). EYLEA HD is growing, but at a lower net selling price, and it has no U.S. regulatory exclusivity, relying solely on patents. Management itself calls the offset of EYLEA declines uncertain. Any Dupixent wobble or new EYLEA HD biosimilar or patent news could pressure the stock. [E3, E6, E7, E9, E11]
- Critic-flagged risk: Other income fell sharply (Q3 $235.9M vs $736.5M; nine months $658.5M vs $1,489.0M), partly offsetting operating gains.
- Critic-flagged risk: Operating expenses are rising (Q3 $3,067.6M vs $2,727.5M), and income tax expense is increasing.
- Critic-flagged risk: EYLEA HD growth was partly offset by a lower net selling price, signaling pricing pressure.
