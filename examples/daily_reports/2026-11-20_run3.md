## 3. PSX: 47.2%

Model 46.7%, debate adjustment +1.0 pts; leaning bull, contested.

- **Thesis (bull, score 7.2):** Refining earnings momentum is accelerating sharply, and that sets up a strong next quarter. Per the 10-Q, the composite 3:2:1 market crack spread averaged $60.17 per barrel in Q3 2026, versus $23.64 in Q3 2025. The filing attributes this to low seasonal global product inventories, particularly diesel, and to geopolitical events reducing global product supply. Realized margin rose to $38.00/BBL in Q3 from $24.08 in Q2 and $10.11 in Q1. As a result, Refining pre-tax income was 6,031 million in Q3 2026, versus (518) million a year earlier. The bull view is that these supply drivers do not fade quickly, so they could keep margins elevated into the next reported quarter. The 10-K risk factors do caution that margins are cyclical and volatile. [E4, E5, E6, E7, E11]
- **Main risk (bear, score 7.5):** Peak-cycle refining earnings are at risk of mean reversion within 90 days. Per the 10-Q, the Q3 2026 composite 3:2:1 crack spread averaged $60.17/bbl versus $23.64 a year earlier. The company attributes this to low seasonal global product inventories, particularly diesel, and to geopolitical events reducing product supply, both of which are transient. Refining pre-tax income was $6,031 million out of $7,108 million total, so the quarter's earnings rest overwhelmingly on one volatile segment. The 10-K itself warns that margins are cyclical and volatile. History shows the downside: refining went from $5,340 million in 2023 to $(365) million in 2024 on lower cracks. If seasonal inventories rebuild or the geopolitical supply disruption eases, consensus could reset lower from a record base. [E4, E7, E10, E11, E3]
