**Risks the critic flagged:**
- Lower product margin rates partially offset Domestic gross profit gains.
- The $34 million IEEPA tariff refunds and the reduction in restructuring charges are likely non-recurring boosts to margins and operating income, which flatters underlying results.
- The International segment deteriorated: the adjusted SG&A rate rose to 20.5% from 19.3%, the operating income rate fell, and foreign exchange had an unfavorable impact.
- The traditional gaming category is declining in both segments, with Domestic Entertainment comparable sales down 6.3%.
- Store count guidance differs: the 10-K expected approximately 4 additional Domestic Best Buy stores, while the 10-Q expects an additional two. The store base is also shrinking through restructuring closures (Yardbird, Outlet and Canada Mobile).
- Rising SG&A pressure comes from employee and incentive compensation, advertising and credit card processing fees.
- The macro backdrop is worsening: rising 10-year and 2-year yields, a higher fed funds rate, widening high-yield spreads, VIX up to 24.56, CPI at 3.7%, and unemployment at 4.1%. All of this may pressure discretionary electronics demand.
- Insider selling of about $38.4 million was not done under Rule 10b5-1 plans, including a large sale by founder Richard Schulze.
- Cost-of-living increases may pressure compensation and benefits, and higher transportation, insurance, healthcare and commodity costs may raise SG&A.
- Tariff volatility makes planning for inventory, pricing and supply chain strategy difficult.
